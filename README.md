# KARIGAR

> **Every hand leaves a story.**

KARIGAR is a digital identity and discovery platform for Indian artisans and living craft traditions.

The idea is simple:

**KARIGAR doesn't just help artisans sell what they make — it helps them build a digital identity.**

A craft is not just a product.

It is connected to:

**Person → Place → Practice → Story → Craft → Discovery → Commerce**

---

## 🎯 What We Are Building

KARIGAR brings together two experiences.

### For people discovering crafts

Users can:

- discover crafts
- discover artisans
- explore traditions and practices
- learn the story behind a craft
- see where it comes from
- see how it is made
- understand the person behind the work
- explore how the artisan wants to sell it
- interact with Saathi

### For artisans

Artisans can:

- introduce themselves
- share their location
- tell their story
- create a craft profile
- upload craft photographs
- describe their work
- share a making/process video
- choose a selling method
- eventually build their KARIGAR ID

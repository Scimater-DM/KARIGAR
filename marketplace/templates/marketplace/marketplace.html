Why KARIGAR?

The craft is valuable. The person behind it should be visible too.

Traditional artisans often face barriers that have little to do with their skill — limited reach, digital barriers and a lack of visibility.

Challenge 01

Limited access to markets

Skilled artisans may depend heavily on local markets or intermediaries to reach customers.

KARIGAR → Connect artisans directly with customers beyond their local geography.
Challenge 02

Digital barriers

Creating and managing a sophisticated online store can be difficult and time-consuming.

KARIGAR → A simple, guided process for creating a craft listing.
Challenge 03

The maker becomes invisible

Customers often see an object without knowing the person, place or tradition behind it.

KARIGAR → Every craft can carry the artisan's story.
Challenge 04

Skills are undervalued

Handmade products can appear expensive when customers cannot see the work behind them.

KARIGAR → Show the process, materials and tradition.
